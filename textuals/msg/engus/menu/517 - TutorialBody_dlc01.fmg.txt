252910;The effects of the Scholar's Analyse skill change depending on skill progress (low, mid, or max) and targets.

When targeting enemies:
Low: Reduce enemy attack power
Mid: Further reduce enemy attack
Max: One-time bonus damage to target

When targeting self:
Low: Raise max stamina
Mid: Further raise max stamina
Max: Temporary one-time damage negation
         + launch wave when hit

When activated on self, the highest progress level within range is applied.

Skill progress is affected by the status of enemies and allies, by observations of certain actions, and types of collected specimens.

N.B. Analyse mode camera speed can be changed in system menu.
